About HES Asia

Founded on engineering, built for healthcare's next decade.

Engineers by training, healthcare specialists by focus — serving Malaysia's hospitals since 1995.

HES Asia was founded in March 2017 by a team of engineers, building on advisory and technical work with private healthcare facilities across the Klang Valley dating back to 1995.

Today we work across Medical Technology Planning, Procurement, and Healthcare Management — with a goal of becoming Malaysia's leading medical solutions hub by 2030, and ASEAN's by 2035.
