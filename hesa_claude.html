About HES Asia

Founded on engineering, built for healthcare's next decade.

Established in March 2017, HES Asia was founded by a team of engineers with a shared drive to bring medical technology expertise to the world. Our roots go back further — to 1995, offering technical and advisory services to private healthcare facilities across the Klang Valley.

Today, we work across Medical Technology Planning, Procurement, and Healthcare Management to become the number one hub for medical solutions in Malaysia by 2030, and across ASEAN by 2035.
