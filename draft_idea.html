About HES Asia

Founded on engineering, built for healthcare's next decade.

Established in March 2017, HES Asia was founded by a team of engineers with a shared drive to bring medical technology expertise to the world. Our story actually begins earlier — in 1995, offering technical and advisory services to private healthcare facilities across the Klang Valley.

Today, we work across three core sectors — Healthcare Recruitment, Project Consultancy, and Digital Solutions — to be the number one hub for medical solutions in Malaysia by 2030, and across ASEAN by 2035.

We focus on sharing our expertise and extensive network in the medical technology sector to deliver outstanding outcomes for our business partners across the healthcare industry.
